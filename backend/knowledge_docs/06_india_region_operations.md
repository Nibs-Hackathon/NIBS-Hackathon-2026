# India and Indian Ocean Region Operations

## Climate and monsoon readiness

Indian coastal facilities should prepare for monsoon rainfall, flooding, drainage loading, lightning, high wind, access disruption, contaminated stormwater, and reduced visibility. Confirm drainage paths, bund capacity, emergency power, roof and seal condition, water ingress protection, weather communications, and critical spares before the seasonal risk window.

## Heat and cooling constraints

High ambient temperature and humidity can reduce air-cooler performance, increase cooling-water demand, affect electrical equipment, and raise heat stress. Review seasonal equipment derating, cooling-tower condition, exchanger approach temperatures, control-room heat-stress planning, and backup utility capacity.

## Coastal corrosion

Arabian Sea, Gulf of Kutch, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean locations face salt deposition and humid coastal exposure. Prioritize corrosion under insulation, external chloride exposure, electrical enclosure integrity, structural steel coatings, marine transfer equipment, flare structures, tank roofs, and idle or intermittently used equipment.

## Cyclone and severe weather

Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean facilities require site-specific cyclone plans covering safe unit state, marine suspension, inventory strategy, personnel shelter, emergency power, communications, flood protection, post-storm inspection, and controlled restart. The site plan and public authority direction are controlling.

## Feedstock, logistics, and utilities

Regional facilities may depend on marine crude receipt, product jetties, cross-country pipelines, road or rail movements, imported feedstocks, and shared power or water infrastructure. Operations planning should expose logistics constraints, berth availability, tank capacity, utility reliability, and critical supplier dependencies.

## Jurisdiction and compliance

Applicable requirements vary by country and state. Consult the controlled legal register, consent conditions, environmental permits, petroleum and explosives requirements, factory and labor requirements, coastal-zone conditions, port rules, and emergency coordination arrangements. Do not infer a legal requirement solely from this operational reference.
