# Maintenance Work Control

This reference provides a practical refinery work-control framework for planning, authorizing, executing, and closing maintenance. Site procedures, permits, isolation standards, and equipment manuals remain authoritative.

## Work Identification and Screening

Record the affected asset, observed condition, operational consequence, urgency, and evidence source. Distinguish corrective work from inspection, preventive maintenance, and improvement work. Screen requests for process-safety, environmental, production, and personnel exposure before assigning priority.

## Planning Readiness

A work package should identify scope, asset boundary, skills, tools, parts, access, scaffolding, lifting, inspection hold points, and expected duration. Confirm drawings and manuals are current. Identify simultaneous operations and dependencies that could invalidate the planned window.

## Isolation and Permit Interface

Define the energy-isolation boundary before execution. Verify process depressurization, draining, purging, electrical isolation, and stored-energy control as applicable. The permit-to-work and lockout/tagout process belongs to the operating site; a generated recommendation must never be treated as authorization.

## Scheduling and Coordination

Coordinate operations, maintenance, inspection, engineering, and safety ownership. Freeze only the near-term schedule necessary for safe execution, while preserving visibility of emerging high-risk defects. Bundle compatible work where it reduces repeated exposure without extending the outage unreasonably.

## Execution and Deviation Control

Conduct the pre-job briefing at the work face. Stop and re-plan when plant condition, scope, isolation, access, weather, or required parts differ from the package. Record as-found condition and material substitutions. Escalate any discovery that changes the safety or containment basis.

## Quality and Return to Service

Complete inspection and test hold points, reinstall guards and protective devices, clear temporary materials, and reconcile isolations. Operations owns return-to-service acceptance. Confirm stable readings after startup and define an enhanced-monitoring period when residual uncertainty remains.

## Closeout and Learning

Capture failure mode, work performed, parts used, test results, downtime, and follow-up actions. Link the work order to the originating alert or incident. Update maintenance strategy only after engineering review of repeat failures or material changes.

## Minimum Evidence for AI Recommendations

An AI-generated work suggestion should cite the asset, triggering signal, trend window, confidence or uncertainty, and source documents. Human review is required before scheduling or field execution. Missing evidence should produce an evidence request, not a fabricated instruction.
